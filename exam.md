## 🧠 Theory (Short & Complete)

The **Hadamard gate** is a single-qubit quantum gate represented by
H = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \ 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix}

It converts a definite state like (|0⟩) into a **superposition state**, where the qubit exists in both (|0⟩) and (|1⟩) simultaneously with equal probability.

**Superposition** is a fundamental property of quantum systems that allows a qubit to be in multiple states at the same time until it is measured.

The **Bloch sphere** is a 3D representation of a single qubit, where:

* (|0⟩) is at the **north pole**
* (|1⟩) is at the **south pole**
* Superposition states lie on the **surface of the sphere**

When a Hadamard gate is applied, the qubit moves from a definite state to a point on the **equator of the Bloch sphere**, representing equal superposition.

# 🧠 Correct Equation

H|0\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|0\rangle + |1\rangle)

---

## 🔹 How to say it (simple)

👉
“Hadamard on zero gives equal superposition of zero and one.”

---

## 🔹 What it means

* Before → only **|0⟩**
* After → **50% |0⟩ + 50% |1⟩**

---

## 🔹 If they ask for |1⟩ also

H|1\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|0\rangle - |1\rangle)
